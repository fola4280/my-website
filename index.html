About

Curious by default.

Student · Maker · Youth communicator · Writer

I'm a student, maker and youth communicator from Cork, Ireland. I like learning by making, with my work moving between coding, technology, engineering, sustainability, writing and creative design.

A lot of my projects start with a real-world problem. I build, test and rethink ideas rather than leaving them as ideas, from ReThread and SkillUp to my multimodal memory project.

Alongside building things, I care about sharing ideas and creating opportunities for other young people. That has led me into student voice, communications, STEM, sustainability and public speaking.
